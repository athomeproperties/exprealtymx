Common questions

Straight answers to what most buyers ask first.

Can foreigners buy property in Playa del Carmen?

Yes. Foreigners can buy in the restricted zone, which includes the coast, through a bank trust called a fideicomiso. The guide explains how it works.

Is the guide really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation. I'll follow up to see if I can help, and you can opt out anytime.

Do I need to be in Mexico to buy?

No. Many of my clients complete the process remotely. We can review properties by video and handle documents digitally where the law allows.

Do you only work with large budgets?

No. I work with a range of budgets. Tell me yours in the form and I'll point you to what makes sense.
