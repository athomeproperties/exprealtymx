Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains what information Craig Verbeck of eXp Realty México ("I," "me") collects through exprealtymx.com and its landing pages, how I use it, and the choices you have.

Information I collect

  • Information you give me: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, and your approximate budget, when you request a guide, fill in a form, book a call or message me.
  • Information collected automatically: technical and usage data such as your browser type, pages viewed, approximate location, and, if you arrived from a Google ad, the ad click ID and campaign details. This is collected using cookies and similar technologies, including the Google tag.

How I use it

  • To send you the guide you requested and to follow up about your property search by email, phone, WhatsApp or text.
  • To answer your questions and arrange calls or property viewings.
  • To measure how well my advertising and website perform, and to improve them.

I do not sell your personal information.

Who I share it with

I share information only with service providers that help me run my business, and only as needed to provide their services. These include the form service that delivers your message to my email, my email, scheduling and CRM providers, and Google, which provides advertising and analytics measurement. I may also share information with others if required by law. If you ask me to connect you with other professionals, such as a notario, attorney or lender, I will share your details only with your agreement.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses cookies and the Google tag to measure advertising results. You can control cookies through your browser settings. You can also manage how Google uses data for ads at adssettings.google.com.

How long I keep it

I keep your information for as long as needed to respond to you and to follow up on your property search, and as required by law. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

Your choices

  • You can opt out of messages at any time by telling me or using the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you.

To do any of these, email me at craig.verbeck@expmexico.mx.

Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
